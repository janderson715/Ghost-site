Convene more than one expert  |  Multi-agent deliberation

Real problems cross domains - and single-agent AI forces a staff to break the problem into pieces, ask separate questions, and stitch the answers together by hand. The stitching is where the mistakes live. The AI Council convenes several specialist agents on the same problem: state your tasking, and the platform recommends a chair agent to organize the effort and specialist seats that each bring their own persona, workflows, and document corpus to the same thread. The council deliberates in front of the operator and returns one verdict - then stands by for the next tasking.

How it works

State the main goal

2 Recommend

GHOST proposes chair + seats

3 The council

Chair and seats. Each seat: its own persona, workflows, corpus - and its own model, chosen per role

4 Deliberate

Challenge and reconcile - dissent stays visible

5 Verdict

One synthesis, all reasoning kept

A standing team - task it again

ONE SEAT, ONE MODEL

Council seats are not clones of a single model. Each seat can run on a different underlying model - frontier or local - so the deliberation carries genuinely different perspectives, not one model agreeing with itself five times.

A STANDING TEAM

Convene once, then keep tasking it. The council is a long-running capability that carries context forward as the mission evolves - the team you assembled yesterday takes today's follow-on tasking without re- briefing.

ADVISORY-ONLY BY DESIGN

The operator keeps the decision. The council explains, assesses, debates, and recommends; the verdict arrives with each seat's reasoning traceable, and dissent recorded rather than hidden.

DEPLOYS YOUR WAY

The Agentic Loop is how a G.H.O.S.T. agent works a tasking the way a trained analyst would: it plans its own lines of inquiry, searches your document corpus and your trusted sources of truth round after round - regulation, doctrine, vendor documentation, standard operating procedures, whatever governs your work - opens and reads the passages that matter, checks what it still lacks, and only then assembles the product, delivered with its sources cited and its assumptions declared.

How it works

One plain-language request

2 Stage

4 Assemble

Built section by section from what it read

5 Deliver

Product, sources, and stated gaps

Refine it in place - the next prompt picks up the whole thread

IT SHOWS ITS WORK

GROUNDED, NOT GUESSED

GAPS STATED, NOT FILLED

Capability 04

Smart Router

Always-on classifier
Policy-set threshold
Reversible redaction

One box. The router decides.  |  Complexity-aware model routing

Local-only AI gives up the hardest reasoning; frontier-only AI gives up control of the data - and of the bill. The work is not uniform, so the answer should not be either. The Smart Router scores every request for reasoning depth before anything moves, applies your policy and spend window, and sends the task to the AI model that actually fits - a small model on your own server for high-volume pattern work, a large model on your own server for full-document analysis, and an off-site frontier model only when the reasoning requires it. When frontier is selected, sensitive values are tokenized at the boundary and rehydrated on return: the reasoning leaves, the data does not.

How it works
1 Request

Operator states the task

2 Classify

Custom-trained classifier scores reasoning depth - always on

Policy, threshold, spend window: sovereignty-weighted, balanced, quality-first, or frontier off - you control where the tokens go
3 Routing proxy

Three AI models, one interface - the operator never picks

4 One model selected

Small AI model on your server: high volume, strict schema, pattern work. Large AI model on your server: full-document context, multi-step comparison. Frontier AI model off-site: long reasoning chains, maximum context

5 Sanitize, rehydrate

PII becomes tokens before the call; the mapping stays in an on-prem vault and is never transmitted

One answer returns complete - nothing sensitive left

THE RIGHT MODEL, AUTOMATICALLY

Nobody picks a model. The classifier runs on every request and the proxy makes the call, so routine work is absorbed locally and frontier reasoning is reserved for the problems that actually need it.

REVERSIBLE REDACTION

Sanitization is not deletion. Names, dates of birth, record numbers, and party names become tokens at the boundary and are restored from a local vault on return - a full-fidelity answer with no exposure.

COST STAYS UNDER YOUR CONTROL

Token spend is where most organizations quietly bleed money on AI. Frontier use is bounded by an envelope you set; when consumption runs past it, the router falls back to local tiers until the window resets - the work continues, the invoice does not climb.

ADVISORY-ONLY BY DESIGN

The router decides where reasoning happens, never what the answer should be. Routing decisions, policy trips, and sanitization steps are visible to the operator, and the human keeps authority over the work.

The Smart Router is the routing layer beneath the G.H.O.S.T. agent roster - every agent inherits it.
