G.H.O.S.T. (Guided-Heuristic On-Prem Support & Troubleshooting) is a commercially available, modular agentic AI platform purpose-built for enterprise environments. It delivers specialized AI agents - pre-configured for your industry - that integrate directly into your existing workflows without demanding a new skillset from your team.

Think of it as hiring a team of domain experts that never sleep, never miss a compliance update, and deliver structured, auditable outputs at the speed of business.

Unlike generic LLM interfaces, G.H.O.S.T. encodes institutional knowledge into every agent. Whether it's regulatory analysis in life sciences, contract review in legal, or network diagnostics in IT, each agent is trained and tuned for your domain.

The platform runs on-premise, in private cloud, or hybrid environments - ensuring your sensitive data never leaves your control.

G.H.O.S.T. for contracts & acquisition intelligence

Accelerate the full acquisition lifecycle - from requirements development to award closeout.

Federal acquisition teams are asked to award faster and document more with a shrinking contracting workforce. Market research, requirements packages, source selections, and post-award oversight all compete for the same analyst hours - stretching PALT and pushing files past audit readiness. G.H.O.S.T.'s Acquisition Agent supports the contracting workforce across every phase of the acquisition lifecycle - delivering structured, defensible outputs that keep every file audit-ready and protest-defensible.

Core acquisition capabilities

Market research & industry analysis

Draft RFIs and Sources Sought notices, consolidate vendor responses, and analyze SAM.gov, FPDS-NG, and USASpending data to identify capable industry sources. G.H.O.S.T. generates structured market research reports and small business set-aside analyses that stand up to review.

Market research reports in minutes, not hours or weeks

Acquisition planning & pre-award documentation

Assemble acquisition plan packages and flag gaps before review. G.H.O.S.T. produces IGCE first drafts, D&F and J&A justifications, and consolidation and bundling determinations - each citation-backed to the FAR and agency supplements for a defensible pre-award file.

Acquisition package prep time cut by 65%

Requirements & solicitation development

Generate PWS and SOW first drafts from program office inputs, develop evaluation criteria mirrored to Sections L and M, and run solicitation readiness checks - including sensitive-data scrubs before industry release and consolidated Q&A and amendment support.

First-draft PWS/SOW in under 2 minutes

Proposal evaluation & source selection support

Support technical evaluation boards with structured consensus report drafting and award decision documentation that is consistency-checked against the evaluation record. Prepare unsuccessful-offeror notices and debriefings on protest-defensible timelines.

Evaluation documentation time cut by 70%

Contract administration & performance oversight

Track contractor deliverables against CDRL matrices, analyze monthly status reports, monitor burn rate and funding runway, and review invoice packages before payment approval. Modification reviews enforce SF-30 and scope discipline; CPARS narratives are drafted from the performance record.

Modification review time reduced by 60%

Contract closeout & audit readiness

Run transition-in and transition-out readiness checks, review closeout packages, and complete final documentation of the contract record. G.H.O.S.T. generates closeout checklists and audit-ready files - ensuring clean contract completion with no outstanding obligations.

Closeout documentation time cut by 55%
